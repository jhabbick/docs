## Get up and running in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Create your free Envoy account using your work email or an SSO provider. No credit card required to start your trial.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Set up your first workspace and connect the third-party tools your team uses — from Slack and Google Workspace to custom integrations via the API.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go">
    Invite your teammates, configure role-based access, and start managing your workspace with full visibility and control.
  </Step>
</Steps>
