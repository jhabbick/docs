This guide walks you through the fastest path from zero to a working Envoy setup. By the end, you'll have a live workspace, at least one teammate onboard, and a successful API call under your belt — giving you a solid foundation to build on.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- A **free Envoy account** — sign up at [envoy.com/signup](https://envoy.com/signup) if you haven't already
- Your **API key** — available in **Settings → API Keys** once your account is created

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [envoy.com/signup](https://envoy.com/signup) and register using your work email address or an SSO provider such as Google or Okta. After signing up, check your inbox for a verification email and click the confirmation link to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first workspace">
    Once you're logged in, click **New Workspace** from the dashboard. Give your workspace a name that reflects its purpose — for example, your team name or project — then choose a visibility setting (private or organization-wide) and click **Create Workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite a team member">
    Open your new workspace and navigate to **Members → Invite**. Enter your teammate's email address, select a role (Owner, Admin, or Member), and click **Send Invite**. They'll receive an email prompting them to join.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your first API call">
    With your API key in hand, run the following command to list your workspaces and confirm everything is working:

    ```bash
    curl https://api.envoy.com/v1/workspaces \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json"
    ```

    Replace `YOUR_API_KEY` with the key from **Settings → API Keys**. A successful response returns a JSON array of your workspaces.
  </Step>
</Steps>
