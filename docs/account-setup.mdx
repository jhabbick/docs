Before you start managing workspaces and inviting teammates, take a few minutes to set up your Envoy account properly. A complete profile and secure account configuration make it easier for your colleagues to recognize you and ensure your organization's data stays protected from day one.

## Sign up for an account

To create your Envoy account, go to [envoy.com/signup](https://envoy.com/signup) and choose one of the following sign-up methods:

- **Email and password** — enter your work email address and choose a strong password
- **Google SSO** — click **Continue with Google** and authorize Envoy using your Google Workspace account
- **SAML SSO** — if your organization has already configured SAML, click **Sign in with SSO** and enter your company domain

<Note>
  After signing up with an email address, Envoy sends a verification email to the address you provided. You must click the confirmation link in that email before you can invite teammates or connect integrations. Check your spam folder if the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes.
</Note>

## Complete your profile

Your profile helps teammates identify you across workspaces and keeps notification preferences aligned with how you work. To update your profile, go to **Settings → Profile**.

The following fields are available:

- **Full name** — enter your first and last name as you'd like them to appear to teammates
- **Avatar** — upload a profile photo (PNG or JPG, minimum 200×200 px) to personalize your account
- **Timezone** — select your local timezone so that scheduled reports, notifications, and audit log timestamps reflect your location correctly
- **Notification preferences** — choose which events trigger email or in-app notifications, such as workspace invitations, member activity, or API key usage alerts

Save your changes by clicking **Update Profile** at the bottom of the page.

## Configure organization settings

If you created a new organization during sign-up, or if you're an Admin of an existing one, you can update organization-wide settings under **Settings → Organization**.

- **Organization name** — the display name shown to all members and in billing communications
- **Slug** — a short, URL-safe identifier used in your Envoy dashboard URL (e.g., `app.envoy.com/your-slug`); changing the slug updates all deep links, so notify your team before making this change
- **Logo** — upload your company logo (PNG or SVG recommended) to brand the experience for your members

Click **Save Organization** to apply any changes.

## Enable two-factor authentication

Envoy strongly recommends enabling two-factor authentication (2FA) to protect your account even if your password is compromised.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open security settings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Security** and click **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Open an authenticator app — such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password — and scan the QR code displayed on screen. If you can't scan the code, click **Enter key manually** to copy the setup key into your app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the setup">
    Enter the six-digit code generated by your authenticator app into the **Verification code** field and click **Verify & Enable**. Envoy confirms that 2FA is active on your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your recovery codes">
    Envoy generates a set of single-use recovery codes. Download or copy these codes and store them somewhere safe — you'll need one if you ever lose access to your authenticator app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If your organization has more than 25 members, consider enforcing SSO instead of relying on individual 2FA. SSO centralizes authentication through your identity provider, simplifies offboarding, and meets the compliance requirements of most enterprise security frameworks. To get started, navigate to **Settings → Security → Single Sign-On**.
</Tip>
