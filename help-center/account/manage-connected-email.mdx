## What you'll accomplish

Manage your Gmail and Microsoft inbox connections from **Account**.

## View connections

Go to **Account** and scroll to **Connected Email Accounts**.

![Connected Email Accounts](/images/account/email-connections.png)

Each connection shows:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Primary** | Your default inbox for outreach |
| **Status** | Active, Reauthorization required, or Disconnected |
| **Connected date** | When the inbox was linked |

If reauthorization is required, a banner at the top of **Account** also prompts you to **Reconnect**.

## Connect an inbox

1. Click **Connect Gmail** or **Connect Microsoft**.
2. Complete the provider authorization flow.

If no inbox is connected:

> No inbox connected yet. Connect Gmail or Microsoft to send project outreach from your own address and sync emails back into Outreach.

A **Connect an inbox to send outreach** banner also appears at the top of **Account** until you connect.

## Replace an inbox

Click **Replace with Gmail** or **Replace with Microsoft** to swap your current connection for a different account.

## Disconnect

1. Click **Disconnect** on the connected account.
2. Confirm when prompted.

After disconnecting, you won't be able to send outreach until you connect again.

## Reconnect after reauthorization

If status shows **Reauthorization required**, click **Reconnect** on the connection card or use the banner at the top of **Account**. See [Inbox reauthorization](/help-center/troubleshooting/inbox-reauthorization).
