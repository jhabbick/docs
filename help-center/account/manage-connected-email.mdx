## What you'll accomplish

Manage your Gmail and Microsoft inbox connections from **Account**.

## View connections

Go to **Account → Connected Email Accounts** (or [Account#email-accounts](https://app.hello-envoy.com/account#email-accounts)).

Each connection shows:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Primary** | Your default inbox for outreach |
| **Status** | Active, Reauthorization required, or Disconnected |
| **Listening** | Whether Envoy is monitoring for replies |
| **Renewal required** | Whether the connection needs renewal |

## Connect an inbox

1. Click **Connect Gmail** or **Connect Microsoft**.
2. Complete the provider authorization flow.

If no inbox is connected:

> No inbox connected yet. Connect Gmail or Microsoft to send project outreach from your account.

## Replace an inbox

Click **Replace with Gmail** or **Replace with Microsoft** to swap your current connection for a different account.

## Disconnect

1. Click **Disconnect** on the connected account.
2. Confirm when prompted.

After disconnecting, you won't be able to send outreach until you connect again.

## Reconnect after reauthorization

If status shows **Reauthorization required**, click the reconnect action to restore permissions. See [Inbox reauthorization](/help-center/troubleshooting/inbox-reauthorization).
