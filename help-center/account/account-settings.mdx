## What you'll learn

The **Account** page centralizes your profile, connected email, default project location, appearance, and privacy preferences on one screen.

## Getting there

Click **Account** in the sidebar, or go to [Account](https://app.hello-envoy.com/account).

![Account settings](/images/account/account-settings.png)

## Alerts at the top

Envoy may show dismissible banners when action is needed:

| Banner | What to do |
|--------|------------|
| **Reconnect Gmail / Microsoft** | Click **Reconnect** to restore inbox authorization |
| **Connect an inbox to send outreach** | Click **Connect Gmail** or **Connect Microsoft** |

Dismiss a banner with the **X** if you've noted the message — it won't block other settings on the page.

## Profile

- **Upload photo** / **Remove uploaded photo** — Set or clear your avatar
- **Primary email** — The email address associated with your account
- **Social account link** — Shows if you signed in with Google or Outlook (Microsoft)

## Default project location

Use a ZIP code or a full address. Envoy prefills this location for every new project, and you can still change it per project.

1. Go to **Account** and find **Default project location**.
2. Enter a ZIP code or full address, then select a suggestion.
3. Click **Save default location**.

![Default project location](/images/account/default-location.png)

## Layout

On desktop, **Profile** and **Connected Email Accounts** appear in the left column. **Appearance**, **Default project location**, and **Data & Privacy** appear in the right column.
