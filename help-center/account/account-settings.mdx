## What you'll learn

The **Account** page centralizes your profile, connected email, security, appearance, and privacy preferences.

## Getting there

Click **Account** in the sidebar, or go to [Account](https://app.hello-envoy.com/account).

## Profile

- **Upload photo** / **Remove uploaded photo** — Set or clear your avatar
- **Primary email** — The email address associated with your account
- **Social account link** — Shows if you signed in with Google or Outlook (Microsoft)

## Quick links from elsewhere
