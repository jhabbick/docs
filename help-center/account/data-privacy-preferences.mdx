## What you'll accomplish

Understand and manage how Envoy uses your data, including optional model training participation.

## During onboarding

After creating your account, Envoy shows **Choose your data preferences** (or **Review our updated Privacy Policy** if policies have changed).

You must:

- Accept the **Terms of Service**
- Acknowledge the **Privacy Policy**

You may optionally opt in with:

> Allow Envoy to use eligible content I submit to improve Envoy models.

This is optional, does not affect access to Envoy, and can be changed later in **Account**. Connected Google or Microsoft mailbox data is always excluded.

![Choose your data preferences](/images/getting-started/consent-preferences.png)

Use **Sign out** in the header if you need to switch to a different Google or Outlook account before continuing.

## Update preferences later

1. Go to **Account** and scroll to the **Data & Privacy** section.
2. Review the model training checkbox and disclosure:

   > Allow Envoy to use my eligible Envoy content to improve Envoy models.

3. Review what's included and excluded:

   | | Content |
   |---|---|
   | **Eligible when enabled** | Envoy-native project inputs, prompts, generated outputs, corrections, ratings, feedback, and de-identified product signals |
   | **Always excluded** | Connected mailbox data, credentials, payment data, direct identifiers, and private third-party communications |

4. Toggle the checkbox and click **Save preference**.

   ![Data & Privacy on Account](/images/account/data-privacy.png)

## Privacy policy updates

If Envoy updates its Privacy Policy materially, you may be prompted to re-acknowledge before continuing. The heading changes to **Privacy update** / **Review our updated Privacy Policy**.

## Mailbox authorization

When connecting Gmail or Microsoft, you authorize Envoy to view email related to your selected contacts, prepare drafts, and send approved messages. This is separate from model training — see [Manage connected email](/help-center/account/manage-connected-email).
