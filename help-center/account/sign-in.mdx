## What you'll accomplish

Sign in to Envoy using the same Google or Outlook account you used to register.

## Before you start

- An existing Envoy account created with **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**

<Note>
  Envoy does not support email-and-password sign-in. If you previously used a password, sign in with Google or Outlook instead, or [contact support](https://app.hello-envoy.com/contact) if you need help accessing your account.
</Note>

## Sign in

1. Go to [Sign in](https://app.hello-envoy.com/login).

2. Click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**.

3. Complete the provider sign-in flow in your browser.

4. If you registered with contacts selected from the homepage, Envoy may route you to finish project setup or go to **Dashboard**.

## Sign out

Click **Logout** in the sidebar.

## Switch accounts

To use a different Google or Outlook account:

1. Click **Logout**.
2. Return to [Sign in](https://app.hello-envoy.com/login).
3. Choose the other provider or select a different account in the Google or Microsoft prompt.
