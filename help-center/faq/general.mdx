Whether you're just getting started or have been using Envoy for a while, you may have questions about how the platform works. This page covers the most frequently asked questions from our customers — from signing up for the first time to understanding where your data lives.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Envoy?">
    Envoy is a SaaS workspace management platform that helps teams organize work, control access, and connect the tools they already use. With Envoy, you can create and manage workspaces, assign roles to team members, integrate with third-party services like Slack, GitHub, Jira, and Salesforce, and automate workflows via the REST API or Zapier. Plans range from a free Starter tier to a fully customizable Enterprise offering.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I sign up for a free account?">
    Signing up for Envoy is quick and free. Navigate to [envoy.com](https://envoy.com) and click **Get Started**. Enter your email address, create a password, and verify your email. Once verified, you'll land in your first workspace and can start inviting team members right away. No credit card is required for the Starter plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a workspace?">
    A workspace is your team's dedicated environment inside Envoy. It contains your projects, members, integrations, and settings. Think of it as a shared hub where your team collaborates. Each workspace has its own role assignments, integration connections, and billing plan. You can belong to multiple workspaces under a single Envoy account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many workspaces can I create?">
    The number of workspaces you can create depends on your plan:

    - **Starter (Free):** 1 workspace
    - **Growth ($49/mo):** Up to 10 workspaces
    - **Enterprise (Custom):** Unlimited workspaces

    If you need to create additional workspaces beyond your plan's limit, you can upgrade at any time from **Settings → Billing**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Envoy on mobile?">
    Yes. Envoy is fully responsive and works in any modern mobile browser on iOS and Android. You can manage workspaces, review notifications, update settings, and collaborate with your team from your phone or tablet without needing a separate app. A dedicated mobile application is on our product roadmap — stay tuned to our changelog for updates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I reset my password?">
    If you've forgotten your password or want to set a new one:

    1. Go to the Envoy login page and click **Forgot password?**
    2. Enter the email address associated with your account.
    3. Check your inbox for a password reset email (it may take a few minutes).
    4. Click the link in the email and follow the prompts to create a new password.

    Reset links are valid for 60 minutes. If yours has expired, simply request a new one. If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact [support@envoy.com](mailto:support@envoy.com).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I change my email address?">
    To update the email address on your account:

    1. Click your avatar in the top-right corner and select **Account Settings**.
    2. Under the **Profile** tab, click **Edit** next to your current email address.
    3. Enter your new email address and confirm it.
    4. A verification link will be sent to your new address — click it to confirm the change.

    Your old email will continue to work until the new one is verified. If you run into issues, reach out to [support@envoy.com](mailto:support@envoy.com).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Envoy have a free plan?">
    Yes. The **Starter plan** is free forever and includes core workspace management features for small teams. Here's a quick overview of all plans:

    | Plan | Price | Best For |
    |------|-------|----------|
    | Starter | Free | Individuals and small teams |
    | Growth | $49/mo | Growing teams needing more workspaces and integrations |
    | Enterprise | Custom | Large organizations with advanced security and compliance needs |

    You can upgrade from the Starter plan at any time from **Settings → Billing** without losing any of your existing data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
    Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Before proceeding, make sure to export any data you want to keep from **Settings → Data Export**.

    To delete your account:

    1. Go to **Account Settings → Advanced**.
    2. Scroll to the **Danger Zone** section and click **Delete Account**.
    3. Type your email address to confirm, then click **Confirm Deletion**.

    If you are the sole Admin of a workspace, you must either transfer ownership to another member or delete the workspace before deleting your account. For assistance, contact [support@envoy.com](mailto:support@envoy.com).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where is my data stored?">
    Envoy stores all customer data in secure, SOC 2-compliant data centers located in the United States. Data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Enterprise customers can request information about data residency options and regional storage preferences as part of their contract. For detailed information on our data handling practices, please review our [Privacy Policy](https://envoy.com/privacy).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
