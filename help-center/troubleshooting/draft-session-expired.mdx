## What you'll accomplish

Understand what happens when a saved search or onboarding draft expires, and how to continue.

## When this happens

Envoy saves your anonymous homepage search in your browser so you can sign up later with your contact selections intact. These drafts expire after a period of inactivity.

You may see this when:

- Registering after an expired search: *Your search is no longer available. Start a new search to continue with those contacts.*
- Opening `/onboarding/project` after expiration — a recovery screen with links to **Dashboard** or a new search

## What to do

### Option 1: Start a new search

1. Go to the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com).
2. Describe your project and search again.
3. Select contacts and continue to registration.

### Option 2: Continue without the old draft

1. Go to **Dashboard**.
2. Click **+ New project** to create a project manually.
3. Add contacts from **Contacts** or use **🔍 Find new contacts** on the project **Overview** tab.

## Browser storage

Drafts are stored locally in your browser (`localStorage`). Clearing browser data for the Envoy site removes saved searches.

<Tip>
  Complete registration soon after selecting contacts to avoid expiration. If you're not ready to sign up, bookmark the homepage and return within a reasonable time.
</Tip>
