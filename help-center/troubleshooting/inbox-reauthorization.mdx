## What you'll accomplish

Restore a disconnected or expired inbox connection so outreach can send again.

## Symptoms

- **Account → Connected Email Accounts** shows **Reauthorization required**
- Outreach messages fail with **Send failed** or **Needs attention**
- **Renewal required** appears on the connection card

## Why this happens

Email providers periodically require you to re-approve permissions. This is normal — your inbox token expired or was revoked.

## Steps to fix

1. Go to **Account → Connected Email Accounts**.

2. Find the connection with **Reauthorization required**.

3. Click the reconnect or reauthorize action (or **Replace with Gmail** / **Replace with Microsoft** if needed).

4. Complete the provider authorization flow in your browser.

5. Confirm status returns to **Active**.

6. Return to **Outreach** and retry any failed messages.

## Prevent future interruptions

- Avoid revoking Envoy's access in your Google or Microsoft account settings unless you intend to disconnect
- Reconnect promptly when you see **Reauthorization required** — delayed reauth may cause missed reply sync

## Still having trouble?

1. **Disconnect** the inbox from **Account**, then connect again fresh.
2. Check that you're authorizing the correct Google or Microsoft account.
3. See [Troubleshoot outreach](/help-center/outreach/troubleshoot-outreach) for send-specific errors.
