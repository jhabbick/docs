## Account and sign-in

### I can't sign in with Google or Outlook

Envoy supports sign-in with **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** only — there is no email-and-password option.

On registration, make sure you checked the mailbox authorization box before clicking the sign-in button.

If buttons are disabled, check the authorization checkbox on the [Create account](https://app.hello-envoy.com/register) page.

### I need to use a different Google or Outlook account

Click **Logout** in the sidebar, then sign in again and choose the correct account in the Google or Microsoft prompt.

### I'm stuck on the consent page

You must accept the Terms of Service and acknowledge the Privacy Policy to continue. Use **Sign out** if you need to switch accounts.

## Contacts and search

### No search results on the homepage

Try broadening your project description or verifying your ZIP/postal code. Click **Search again** with updated details.

### My pre-signup contacts didn't carry over

Your anonymous search session may have expired. Start a new search on the homepage and select contacts again before registering.

### Contact missing email

Add an email address on the project **Overview** tab or in **Contacts** before sending outreach.

## Projects

### I don't see my project in the sidebar

Only active projects appear in the sidebar list (up to 10). Open **Dashboard** to see recent projects.

### Unsaved changes warning

When editing on the **Overview** tab, save with **Save** before switching tabs or you'll be prompted to confirm.
