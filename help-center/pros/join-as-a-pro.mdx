## What you'll learn

How service providers (**Pros**) join Envoy and what happens while an account is pending approval.

<Note>
  The Pro experience is expanding. This guide covers registration and the pending approval screen available today.
</Note>

## Who should register as a Pro

Register as a Pro if you're a service provider — contractor, caterer, electrician, planner, or other professional — who wants to receive project inquiries through Envoy.

Consumers planning projects should register as a consumer instead. See [Create your account](/help-center/get-started/create-your-account).

## Register

1. Go to [Create your Pro account](https://app.hello-envoy.com/register?accountType=vendor).

2. Check the mailbox authorization box, then click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** (same flow as consumer registration).

3. After sign-in, Envoy redirects you to the pending approval page.

## Pending approval

While your account is under review, you'll see **You're on the list** with this guidance:

### What happens next?

1. Our team reviews your profile to confirm you're a legitimate professional or business.
2. Once approved, you'll receive an email with next steps to complete your profile.
3. After that, Envoy can start routing project inquiries your way.

![Pro pending approval](/images/account/vendor-pending.png)

## If approval needs more information

If your application isn't approved yet, the page shows **We need more information** and prompts you to [Get in touch](https://app.hello-envoy.com/contact) so the team can help verify your business.

## Switch account type

On the registration page, use **Register as a Consumer** if you signed up as a Pro by mistake.
