## What you'll accomplish

Find and select relevant contacts for your project on the Envoy homepage — no account required. After you sign in, you can also search during project creation from **Dashboard**.

## Before you start

- A clear description of what you're planning — be specific about the work (e.g., *garden wedding for 120 guests with florist and catering* rather than *wedding*)
- Your ZIP or postal code (e.g., `29401` or `M5H 2N2`)

## Steps

1. Go to the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com).

2. In **What are you planning?**, describe your project. For example: *Outdoor garden wedding for 120 guests in Charleston this June…*

   <img noZoom className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/getting-started/landing-intake.png" alt="Landing intake form" />
   <img noZoom className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/getting-started/landing-intake-dark.png" alt="Landing intake form" />

   <Tip>
     Try one of the example prompt chips below the text field to get started quickly.
   </Tip>

3. Enter your **ZIP or postal code** in the location field.

4. Click **Search**. Envoy groups results by category under **Contacts for your project**.

5. Review each contact card. Pay attention to badges like **Onboarded to Envoy** or **Unverified listing** — see [Contact badges explained](/help-center/contacts/contact-badges-explained) for details.

   <img noZoom className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/getting-started/search-results-badges.png" alt="Search results with badges" />
   <img noZoom className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/getting-started/search-results-badges-dark.png" alt="Search results with badges" />

6. Select the contacts you want to reach out to. You can use **Select all** or **Deselect all**, or tap individual cards.

   <Snippet file="snippets/max-contacts-note.mdx" />

7. Click **Continue with N contact(s)** to proceed to account creation. Your selections are saved for registration.

## If no results appear

If you see **No matches found for your search**, try:

- Being more specific about the work you need — vague descriptions like *fix my house* may return no matches
- Checking that your ZIP or postal code is correct
- Clicking **Search again** with adjusted details

Envoy matches contacts to the type of work you describe. A specific project description works better than a very short or generic one.

## Returning later

Envoy saves your search progress in your browser. When you return to the homepage, your previous search and selections may restore automatically.

If your saved search has expired, you'll see a message when registering. Start a new search and select contacts again.

## From the dashboard

Already have an account? You don't need to start on the homepage.

1. From **Dashboard**, click **+ New project** or **New Project** in the sidebar.
2. Complete **Essentials** (title, description, and location).
3. On the **Contacts** step, Envoy searches automatically. Select contacts, or click **Refine search** to adjust the description or location and **Search again**.

See [Create a project](/help-center/projects/create-a-project) for the full wizard steps.
