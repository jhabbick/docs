## What you'll accomplish

Create your first project with the post-registration wizard so Envoy can help you plan and reach out to your selected contacts.

## Before you start

- A completed Envoy account and consent preferences
- Contacts selected during homepage search (optional — you can add contacts later)

## The 4-step wizard

After registration, Envoy opens the project wizard at `/onboarding/project`. The steps are:

### 1. Essentials

- **Project title** — A short name for your project
- **Description** — What you're planning (often prefilled from your homepage search)

If any selected contacts are missing email addresses, Envoy shows an **Additional contact details required** section:

- Enter an email for each contact, or click **Remove** to drop them from the project
- You can't continue until every remaining contact has a valid email

Click **Continue** to proceed.

![Project wizard Essentials step](/images/getting-started/project-wizard.png)

### 2. Details (Where & When)

- **Location** — Search and select an address (autocomplete powered by location search)
- **Start date**, **End date**, and **Deadline** — Optional timeline fields

Click **Continue**, or **Skip & create** to finish with just the essentials filled in.

### 3. Budget

- **Amount** — Your estimated budget
- **Currency** — Common currencies like USD, EUR, and GBP appear at the top of the list

Click **Continue**, or **Skip & create**.

### 4. Goals

- Describe what success looks like for this project in free text

Click **Create project** to finish.

## After creation

Envoy opens your new project page with the **Overview**, **Chat**, and **Outreach** tabs ready to use.

## Skip ahead

On the **Details**, **Budget**, and **Goals** steps, you can click **Skip & create** (or **Skip & create project** from the dashboard wizard) if you want to fill in details later from the **Overview** tab.

## If your session expired

If your pre-registration search expired, you'll see a recovery message instead of the wizard. From there you can:

- Go to **Dashboard** and create a project manually
- Start a new contact search from the homepage
