## What you'll accomplish

Create an Envoy account with Google or Outlook and complete the steps needed to start managing projects and sending outreach.

## Before you start

- A **Google** or **Outlook (Microsoft)** account
- If you searched for contacts on the homepage, click **Continue with N contact(s)** first so your selections carry over to registration

<Note>
  Envoy does not offer email-and-password sign-up. Use **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** on the registration page.
</Note>

## Steps

1. Go to [Create account](https://app.hello-envoy.com/register) or follow the link from the homepage after selecting contacts.

2. Check the mailbox authorization box:

   > I authorize Envoy to view my email, prepare local drafts, and send approved messages from my connected account.

   Envoy only views emails sent and received by contacts you select. You can disconnect your inbox anytime from **Account**.

3. Click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** to sign in with your Google or Outlook account.

4. Complete the provider sign-in flow in your browser.

   ![Registration page](/images/getting-started/registration-form.svg)

<Warning>
  You must check the mailbox authorization box before social sign-in. Without it, the **Continue with Google** and **Continue with Microsoft** buttons stay disabled.
</Warning>

## After registration

Envoy guides you through two more steps:

1. **Choose your data preferences** — Accept the Terms of Service, acknowledge the Privacy Policy, and optionally opt in to model training. See [Data & privacy preferences](/help-center/account/data-privacy-preferences).

2. **Complete your first project** — If you selected contacts before signing up, you'll land on the project wizard with your details prefilled. See [Complete your first project](/help-center/get-started/complete-your-first-project).

## Register as a Pro

Service providers should register at [Create your Pro account](https://app.hello-envoy.com/register?accountType=vendor). See [Join as a Pro](/help-center/pros/join-as-a-pro).

## Already have an account?

Go to [Sign in](https://app.hello-envoy.com/login) and use **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**.
