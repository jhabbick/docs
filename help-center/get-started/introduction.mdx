Envoy is a SaaS platform designed for operations, IT, and engineering teams who need a single place to manage workspaces, control access, and connect the tools their organization depends on. Instead of juggling disconnected tools and manual processes, Envoy brings workspace management, third-party integrations, a powerful REST API, and enterprise-grade security under one roof — so your team can focus on work instead of infrastructure.

## What you can do with Envoy

- **Manage workspaces** — create, configure, and archive workspaces as your team's needs evolve
- **Invite and organize teammates** — add members, assign roles, and group people by team or function
- **Connect third-party tools** — set up integrations with Slack, Google Workspace, Okta, and more without writing custom glue code
- **Automate with the API** — use the REST API to provision resources, trigger workflows, and sync data with your own systems
- **Receive real-time events** — subscribe to webhooks and react to changes in your workspace the moment they happen
- **Export and report** — pull usage data and activity logs into your reporting tools or data warehouse
- **Enforce security policies** — require SSO, enable 2FA, review audit trails, and manage billing seats from one place
