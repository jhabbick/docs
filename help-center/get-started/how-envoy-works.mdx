## What you'll learn

The end-to-end flow for using Envoy — from your first search to sending outreach emails.

## The full journey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discover contacts">
    On the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com), describe what you're planning and enter your ZIP or postal code — no account required. You can select up to 8 contacts before signing up. After you're signed in, you can also search during project creation from **Dashboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Continue with your selected contacts to register. Complete consent preferences, then finish setting up your first project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your project">
    Use the two-step wizard — **Essentials** and **Contacts** — to name the project, set a location, and attach the contacts you selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage from your dashboard">
    Return to **Dashboard** anytime to open projects, browse **Contacts**, or adjust **Account** settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan in Chat">
    Open a project and switch to the **Chat** tab. Ask questions, clarify scope, and update details like budget or deadline. When planning is complete, Envoy prepares outreach drafts automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send outreach">
    Connect Gmail or Microsoft from **Account**, then open the **Outreach** tab to review drafts, attach files, revise with Envoy if needed, and send emails to your contacts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Inside a project

Every project has three tabs:

| Tab | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **Overview** | View and edit project details; attach, add, or find contacts |
| **Chat** | Envoy planning assistant scoped to this project |
| **Outreach** | Draft, revise, send, and reply to emails with contacts |

## Navigation at a glance

Once you're signed in, the sidebar gives you quick access to:

- **Dashboard** — Your home base and recent projects
- **Contacts** — Your global contacts directory
- **New Project** — Start the project creation wizard
- **Projects** — Jump directly to any active project
- **Help Center** — These docs, opened in a new tab
- **Account** — Profile, connected email, default location, appearance, and privacy

<Tip>
  You don't have to follow every step in order. Many users search for contacts first, then sign up. Others create an account and find contacts during project creation from **Dashboard**, or add them later from the **Contacts** page.
</Tip>
