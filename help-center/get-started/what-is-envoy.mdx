## What you'll learn

Envoy is a project management and outreach platform for anyone coordinating real-world projects — home renovations, events, commercial build-outs, and more.

## The problem Envoy solves

Planning a project usually means:

- Searching for the right service providers
- Keeping track of who you've contacted
- Writing and sending introductory emails
- Following up as replies come in

Envoy brings these steps together so you can move from "I need to get this done" to "I've reached out to the right people" faster.

## What Envoy does for you

1. **Discover contacts** — Describe your project and location. Envoy surfaces relevant service providers grouped by category.
2. **Organize your project** — Capture details like location, timeline, budget, and goals in one place.
3. **Plan with AI** — Use the **Chat** tab to refine scope, priorities, and next steps with a project-scoped assistant.
4. **Send outreach** — Draft, review, and send emails to contacts from your connected Gmail or Microsoft inbox.

## Who Envoy is for

Envoy is built for **consumers** — homeowners, small business owners, event planners, and anyone who needs to coordinate multiple service providers for a project.

If you're a service provider looking to receive project inquiries through Envoy, see [Join as a Pro](/help-center/pros/join-as-a-pro).

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Contacts** | Service providers you add to a project or your contacts directory |
| **Project** | A hub for one initiative (e.g., a kitchen remodel or company offsite) |
| **Outreach** | Email messages Envoy drafts for you to review and send to contacts |
| **Pro** | A service provider account type on Envoy |

<Note>
  Envoy sends outreach from **your** email address. You stay in control — review every message before it's sent.
</Note>
