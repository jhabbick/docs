## What you'll learn

**Outreach** is how you email contacts about your project. Envoy drafts messages for you; you review and approve every send.

## How outreach fits in

Outreach lives on the **Outreach** tab inside each project. To use it, you need:

1. **Contacts attached** to the project (from the **Overview** tab)
2. **A connected inbox** — Gmail or Microsoft (from **Account**)

## Drafts from Chat

If you finish planning on the **Chat** tab, Envoy may already have generated drafts for your attached contacts. Open **Outreach** to review them — you don't always need to start with **New message**.

When Chat completes planning, it tells you drafts are being prepared and directs you to the **Outreach** tab.

## The outreach workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your email">
    Link Gmail or Microsoft from **Account → Connected Email Accounts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach contacts">
    Add contacts on the project **Overview** tab so they appear in outreach.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review or create a draft">
    Check **Outreach** for drafts from Chat planning. To start fresh, click **New message**, choose a contact, and click **Create draft**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and send">
    Read the draft, revise with Envoy if needed, then click **Approve & Send**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply to responses">
    When contacts reply, view the thread and send replies from the same tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Message statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Draft** | Envoy-generated message ready for your review |
| **Pending review** | Draft is being prepared or updated |
| **Sent** | Message was delivered from your inbox |
| **Received** | A reply arrived from the contact |
| **Send failed** | Delivery failed — use **Retry** to try again |
| **Needs attention** | Something requires your action (e.g., a failed send or reauthorization) |

## Simple vs Detailed

When viewing or revising messages, toggle between **Simple** and **Detailed** to control how much project context appears in the draft.
