## What you'll accomplish

Refine an outreach draft with AI before sending — adjust tone, add details, or change length.

## Before you start

- An open draft on the **Outreach** tab (after clicking **Create draft**)

## Revise a draft

1. With a draft open, click **Revise**.

2. Enter instructions in the text field. For example:
   - *Tighten the tone*
   - *Add missing project details*
   - *Shorten the message*

3. Click the revise action button to generate an updated draft.

4. Review the new version. Click **Approve & Send** when ready, or revise again.

5. Click **Cancel** to close the revise panel without applying changes.

## Simple vs Detailed

Use the **Simple** and **Detailed** toggles to control how much project context Envoy includes in the message:

| Style | Best for |
|-------|----------|
| **Simple** | Short, direct introductions |
| **Detailed** | Messages that need full project context (timeline, budget, goals) |

You can switch styles before or during revision.

## Cancel or retry

- **Cancel draft** — Remove the draft entirely
- **Retry draft generation** — Start over if the initial draft failed to generate

<Tip>
  Be specific in your revise instructions. "Mention the March deadline and ask for availability" works better than "make it better."
</Tip>
