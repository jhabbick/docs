## What you'll accomplish

Diagnose and fix the most common outreach problems.

## Connect an inbox to send outreach

**Symptom:** Empty state on **Account** or **Outreach** saying you need to connect an inbox.

**Fix:**

1. Go to **Account → Connected Email Accounts**.
2. Click **Connect Gmail** or **Connect Microsoft**.
3. Complete authorization and return to **Outreach**.

See [Connect your email](/help-center/outreach/connect-your-email).

## Your linked contacts do not have email

**Symptom:** Outreach tab shows that contacts are linked but lack email addresses.

**Fix:**

1. Open the **Overview** tab.
2. Edit the contact or add a new one with a valid email address.
3. Return to **Outreach** and try **New message** again.

## No contacts linked

**Symptom:** *No contacts are linked to this project yet. Add one in the Overview tab…*

**Fix:** Attach or add contacts from the **Overview** tab. See [Attach contacts to projects](/help-center/contacts/attach-contacts-to-projects).

## Send failed

**Symptom:** Message status shows **Send failed** or a thread shows **Needs attention**.

**Fix:**

1. Check that your inbox status is **Active** on **Account**.
2. If status is **Reauthorization required**, reconnect your inbox — see [Inbox reauthorization](/help-center/troubleshooting/inbox-reauthorization).
3. Click **Retry** on the failed draft or message.

## Draft generation failed

**Symptom:** Draft doesn't appear or shows an error after **Create draft**.

**Fix:**

1. Confirm the contact has an email address and is attached to the project.
2. Click **Retry draft generation**.
3. If the problem persists, try **Cancel draft** and start a new message.

## Reauthorization required

**Symptom:** **Account** shows **Reauthorization required** for your connected inbox.

**Fix:** See [Inbox reauthorization](/help-center/troubleshooting/inbox-reauthorization).
