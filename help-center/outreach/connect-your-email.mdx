## What you'll accomplish

Connect a Gmail or Microsoft inbox so you can send outreach from your own email address.

## Before you start

- An Envoy account with completed consent preferences
- Access to the Gmail or Microsoft account you want to connect

## Steps

1. Open **Account** from the sidebar (or go to [Account](https://app.hello-envoy.com/account)).

2. Scroll to **Connected Email Accounts**.

3. Click **Connect Gmail** or **Connect Microsoft**.

   <img noZoom className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/account/email-connections.png" alt="Connected email accounts" />
   <img noZoom className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/account/email-connections-dark.png" alt="Connected email accounts" />

4. Complete the provider authorization flow in your browser.

5. When connected, your account shows status details:

   | Status | Meaning |
   |--------|---------|
   | **Active** | Inbox is connected and ready to send |
   | **Reauthorization required** | Permissions expired — click to reconnect |
   | **Disconnected** | Inbox was disconnected |

6. The first connected inbox is marked **Primary**. Outreach sends from your primary inbox by default.

## Replace or add another inbox

- **Replace with Gmail** / **Replace with Microsoft** — Swap your current connection for a different provider account
- **Disconnect** — Remove the inbox connection (you'll be asked to confirm)

<Tip>
  Envoy only accesses emails related to contacts you've selected. You can disconnect at any time from **Account**.
</Tip>

## From the Outreach tab

If you open **Outreach** before connecting an inbox, you'll see **Connect an inbox to send outreach**. Click **Manage Email Accounts** to go directly to **Account**.
