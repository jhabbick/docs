## What you'll accomplish

Read replies from contacts and send follow-up messages from within your project's **Outreach** tab.

## Before you start

- At least one sent outreach message that received a reply
- A connected and active inbox

## View a thread

1. Open your project and go to the **Outreach** tab.

2. Select a thread from the list. You'll see the full conversation — outbound messages you sent and inbound replies from the contact.

3. Threads with issues show a **Needs attention** badge. Common reasons include send failures or inbox reauthorization requirements.

Files on a message appear under the body. Click a file to download it — see [Attach files to outreach](/help-center/outreach/attach-files).

## Send a reply

1. Open the thread you want to respond to.

2. Type your reply in the composer, or click **Write with AI** to draft with Envoy first.

3. Optionally [attach files](/help-center/outreach/attach-files).

4. Click **Send reply**. If files are still uploading, you'll see **Waiting for uploads...**.

## Revise a reply with Envoy

Before sending, you can ask Envoy to improve your reply:

1. Click **Write with AI** in the reply composer.

2. In **What should Envoy change?**, enter instructions (e.g., *Confirm the site visit for next Tuesday*).

3. Click **Generate draft**. While Envoy works, the button shows **Writing...**.

4. Review the **Revision preview**. Click **Apply revision** to use it, or **Keep current draft** to discard the suggestion.

5. Click **Send reply** when ready.

## Activity timeline

Each thread shows message history with timestamps. If a thread has no messages yet, you'll see **No messages yet**. For projects with no outreach at all, you'll see **No threads yet**.
