## What you'll accomplish

Create a new project from the dashboard with the Essentials → Details → Budget → Goals wizard.

## Before you start

- A signed-in Envoy account

## Steps

1. From **Dashboard**, click **+ New project** or **Create your first project**, or click **New Project** in the sidebar.

2. Complete the wizard steps:

   ### Essentials
   - **Title** — Name your project
   - **Description** — What you're planning

   ### Details (Where & When)
   - **Location** — Search and select an address
   - **Start date**, **End date**, **Deadline**

   ### Budget
   - **Amount** and **Currency**

   ### Goals
   - Free-text goals for the project

   ![Project wizard](/images/projects/project-wizard.svg)

3. Optionally, click **🔍 Find new contacts for this project** during creation to search for and attach contacts before finishing.

4. Click **Create project** on the final step, or **Skip & create project** on later steps to finish early.

## Draft auto-save

The wizard saves your progress to your browser as you type. If you leave and return, your draft may restore automatically.

## After creation

Envoy opens your new project on the **Overview** tab. From there, attach contacts, use **Chat**, or start **Outreach**.
