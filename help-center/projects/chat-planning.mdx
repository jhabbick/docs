## What you'll accomplish

Use Envoy on the **Chat** tab to refine plans, update project details, and prepare outreach for your contacts.

## Before you start

- An open project
- Contacts attached on the **Overview** tab (required before Envoy can generate outreach drafts)

## Steps

1. Open your project and click the **Chat** tab.

2. On your first visit, Envoy shows a greeting to help you get started.

3. Type your question or request in the message field at the bottom.

4. Press **Send**. Envoy responds with suggestions scoped to **this project** — using your title, description, location, budget, deadline, and other details.

   <img noZoom className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/projects/project-chat.png" alt="Project Chat" />
   <img noZoom className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/projects/project-chat-dark.png" alt="Project Chat" />

5. Continue the conversation to explore options, clarify requirements, or plan next steps.

You can keep typing while waiting for a response — the message field stays active.

Replies may include short lists or tables when Envoy is comparing options.

## What Chat is (and isn't)

**Chat is for project planning** — scope, timeline, priorities, and preparation for outreach. It knows your project context.

**Chat can also explain how things work.** Ask about timelines, how contractors quote, what to expect from contacts, or what to prioritize. Envoy answers those questions in the context of your project.

**Chat is not** a general-purpose assistant. Questions unrelated to your project may get redirected back to project planning.

## Update project details from Chat

Ask Envoy to change the same fields you can edit on **Overview**. Envoy saves the change and the **Overview** tab updates.

You can update:

- **Title** and **description**
- **Location** — share a ZIP code or address
- **Deadline** — natural language is fine (*end of the week*, *in a month*, *ASAP*)
- **Budget** — amounts like *10k*, *around ten thousand*, or *$10,000*

If Envoy needs more information, it will ask before saving.

You can still edit these fields directly on **Overview** — see [Edit or delete a project](/help-center/projects/edit-or-delete-a-project).

## When planning is complete

After you and Envoy align on the project and what to ask for, Chat moves to a final check:

1. Envoy asks whether you have any last details to add.
2. If you're ready, Envoy **automatically generates outreach drafts** for contacts attached to the project.
3. Chat tells you drafts are ready — switch to the **Outreach** tab to review, revise, and send.

If you're not ready yet, say so. You can keep gathering details and generate drafts later.

If no contacts are attached yet, Chat explains that you need to add contacts on the **Overview** tab before drafts can be prepared.

<Note>
  You don't need to click **New message** on Outreach if Chat already generated drafts — open **Outreach** and review what's there first.
</Note>

## After drafts exist

You can keep using **Chat** after outreach drafts are ready. Ask Envoy to refresh or create new drafts when your scope, contacts, or details change. If you update the project without asking for new drafts, Envoy may check whether you want to regenerate them.

## Tips for good results

- Reference specific details: dates, budget, location, or contacts
- Ask process questions: *How do electricians usually scope a panel upgrade?*
- Ask for recommendations when you're unsure — saying *I don't know* or *you decide* is fine
- Attach contacts on **Overview** before finishing planning so drafts generate automatically

<Tip>
  Use Chat to clarify what you want to say, then review auto-generated drafts on **Outreach**.
</Tip>
