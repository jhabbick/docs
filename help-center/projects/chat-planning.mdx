## What you'll accomplish

Use Envoy on the **Chat** tab to refine plans, clarify scope, and prepare outreach for your contacts.

## Before you start

- An open project
- Contacts attached on the **Overview** tab (required before Envoy can generate outreach drafts)

## Steps

1. Open your project and click the **Chat** tab.

2. On your first visit, Envoy shows a greeting to help you get started.

3. Type your question or request in the message field at the bottom.

4. Press **Send**. Envoy responds with suggestions scoped to **this project** — using your title, description, goals, and other details.

   ![Project Chat](/images/projects/project-chat.png)

5. Continue the conversation to explore options, clarify requirements, or plan next steps.

You can keep typing while waiting for a response — the message field stays active.

## What Chat is (and isn't)

**Chat is for project planning** — scope, timeline, priorities, and preparation for outreach. It knows your project context.

**Chat can also explain how things work.** Ask about timelines, how contractors quote, what to expect from vendors, or what to prioritize. Envoy answers those questions in the context of your project.

**Chat is not** a general-purpose assistant. Questions unrelated to your project may get redirected back to project planning.

## When planning is complete

After you and Envoy align on goals, vendors, and what to ask for, Chat moves to a final check:

1. Envoy asks whether you have any last details to add.
2. On your next reply, Envoy **automatically generates outreach drafts** for contacts attached to the project.
3. Chat tells you drafts are ready — switch to the **Outreach** tab to review, revise, and send.

If no contacts are attached yet, Chat explains that you need to add contacts on the **Overview** tab before drafts can be prepared.

<Note>
  You don't need to click **New message** on Outreach if Chat already generated drafts — open **Outreach** and review what's there first.
</Note>

## Tips for good results

- Reference specific details: dates, budget, location, or contacts
- Ask process questions: *How do electricians usually scope a panel upgrade?*
- Ask for recommendations when you're unsure — saying *I don't know* or *you decide* is fine
- Attach contacts on **Overview** before finishing planning so drafts generate automatically

<Tip>
  Use Chat to clarify what you want to say, then review auto-generated drafts on **Outreach**.
</Tip>
