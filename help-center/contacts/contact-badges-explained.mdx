## What you'll learn

Badges on contact cards help you understand how much Envoy knows about each listing before you reach out.

## Badge reference

| Badge | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Onboarded to Envoy** | This contact has a verified listing on Envoy. The business has joined or confirmed their presence on the platform. |
| **Unverified listing** | This listing was created or managed by a consumer and has **not** been verified by the business itself. Proceed with normal due diligence. |
| **Additional contact details required** | The listing is missing an email address. Add an email before you can send outreach. |
| **Saved to Contacts** | You've already saved this contact to your directory (shown during search flows). |

## On search results

When you search on the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com) or during project creation from **Dashboard**, contact cards show badges beneath the business name. Results are grouped by category (e.g., Electrician, Caterer).

![Search results with badges](/images/getting-started/search-results-badges.svg)

## Ownership warnings

Some listings display an ownership warning explaining that the listing is consumer-managed. This is the same situation as **Unverified listing** — the business hasn't confirmed the listing on Envoy.

## Why badges matter

Badges don't block you from selecting or contacting someone. They help you set expectations:

- **Onboarded to Envoy** — Higher confidence the business is reachable through the platform
- **Unverified listing** — You may be the first to connect; verify details independently
- **Missing email** — You'll need to supply contact info before outreach works
