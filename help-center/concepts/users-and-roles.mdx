Envoy uses a role-based access control (RBAC) model to determine what each user can see and do within your organization and its workspaces. Every user who accepts an invitation is assigned exactly one role per workspace — **Admin**, **Member**, or **Viewer** — and that role governs every action they can take. Getting roles right from the start keeps your data secure, reduces accidental changes, and ensures the right people always have the access they need without over-provisioning permissions.

## Role definitions

| Role | Who it's for | What they can do |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Admin** | Workspace owners, IT, team leads who need full control | Manage billing, configure settings and SSO, invite and remove members, manage integrations, view and export all reports, and perform every action available to lower roles. |
| **Member** | Day-to-day contributors who create and maintain content | Create and edit workspace content, manage integrations, invite new members (but not promote anyone to Admin), and view reports. |
| **Viewer** | Stakeholders, auditors, or guests who only need visibility | Read-only access to workspaces and data. Viewers cannot create, modify, or delete any resource. |

## Assign a role

You can assign or change a member's role from the workspace Members page at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Members settings">
    Inside the relevant workspace, open **Settings → Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the member">
    Use the search bar to locate the member by name or email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the role dropdown">
    Click the current role badge next to the member's name to open the role selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the new role">
    Choose **Admin**, **Member**, or **Viewer** from the dropdown. The change takes effect immediately — no confirmation email is sent to the member.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Before demoting an Admin to a lower role, ensure at least one other Admin remains in the workspace. A workspace with zero Admins cannot be managed through the dashboard, and you will need to contact Envoy Support to regain administrative access.
</Warning>

## Custom roles (Enterprise)

Enterprise plans unlock **Custom Roles**, which let you build a named role from a granular list of individual permissions. Custom roles are defined at the organization level and can be applied to any workspace, giving large teams the flexibility to model complex access patterns — such as a "Billing Viewer" who can see invoices but cannot change subscription settings — without granting full Admin access.

To create a custom role, navigate to **Organization Settings → Roles → New Custom Role**.

| Action | Admin | Member | Viewer |
|--------|:-----:|:------:|:------:|

| Manage members | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Configure SSO | ✓ | — | — |
| Create workspaces | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Edit integrations | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| View reports | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Export data | ✓ | ✓ | — |
