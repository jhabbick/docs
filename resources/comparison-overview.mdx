Choosing the right platform is one of the most consequential decisions your team will make. Legacy platforms often come with rigid architectures, opaque pricing, and security features locked behind expensive enterprise tiers. Point solutions, on the other hand, force you to manage a growing stack of specialized tools that rarely talk to each other well. Envoy takes a different approach: a single, composable platform with a developer-friendly API, enterprise security built in, and pricing that's transparent at every tier.

## What Sets Envoy Apart

### REST API Coverage

Envoy's REST API exposes the full surface area of the platform — not a subset reserved for paying enterprise customers. Every resource, every action, and every event is accessible via a well-documented, versioned API from the Starter plan up. This means your developers can start building integrations on day one, and those integrations will continue to work as you upgrade plans without hitting hidden capability walls.

### Integration Breadth

With over 100 native integrations, Envoy connects to the tools your team already depends on: **Slack** for notifications and workflow triggers, **GitHub** for developer-centric automation, **Jira** for issue tracking and project sync, **Salesforce** for CRM-connected workflows, and **Zapier** to bridge Envoy with virtually any other SaaS product in your stack. Unlike point solutions that integrate deeply with only one or two tools, or legacy platforms that bolt on a shallow integration marketplace as an afterthought, Envoy's integrations are first-class and maintained by the Envoy team.

### Security Posture

Envoy is **SOC 2 Type II certified**, with SAML and OIDC-based single sign-on, granular role-based access control, and comprehensive audit logging available to all paid customers — not just those on a top-tier enterprise contract. This means smaller teams and growth-stage companies can meet the security requirements of their enterprise customers and prospects without purchasing a plan that's sized for a company ten times their size.
