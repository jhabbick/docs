Envoy offers three plans designed to grow with your team — from a solo founder exploring the platform on the free Starter tier, to a scaling business on Growth, to a large enterprise that needs custom contracts, dedicated support, and the highest security controls. The guide below breaks down exactly what each plan includes so you can make a confident, informed decision without having to read the fine print.

## Plan Comparison

| Feature | Starter | Growth | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Free forever | $49 / month | Custom (contact sales) |
| **Workspaces** | 1 | Up to 10 | Unlimited |
| **Members per workspace** | Up to 5 | Up to 50 | Unlimited |

| **Support** | Community & docs | Priority email (< 24 hr) | Dedicated success manager |

*All paid plans include a 14-day free trial. Annual billing saves 20% — see the note below.*

## When to Choose Starter

The **Starter plan** is the right choice if you're evaluating Envoy, building a side project, or running a very small team that doesn't yet need enterprise controls. It gives you full access to the REST API and webhooks, one workspace with up to five members, and a core set of integrations to get up and running quickly.

Starter is ideal for:
- **Individual developers** prototyping integrations or building personal automation workflows.
- **Early-stage startups** that want to validate Envoy fits their stack before committing budget.
- **Teams testing a migration** from an existing tool who need a no-risk sandbox environment.

There's no time limit on the Starter plan — it's free forever. When your team or requirements outgrow it, upgrading to Growth takes seconds.

## When to Choose Growth

The **Growth plan** at $49/month is built for teams that have moved past the evaluation stage and need the full Envoy feature set — without the complexity or cost of an enterprise contract. Growth unlocks the complete integration library, SAML/OIDC SSO for secure single sign-on, audit logs, custom roles, and a 99.9% uptime SLA with priority email support.

Growth is ideal for:
- **Scaling startups and SMBs** that need SSO and audit logs to satisfy customer security questionnaires or internal compliance requirements.
- **Engineering teams** that need higher API rate limits and the full integration library to power production workflows.
- **Ops and IT teams** that require role-based access control to enforce least-privilege policies across workspaces.
- **Teams with multiple workspaces** (up to 10) managing different departments, clients, or environments.

## When to Choose Enterprise

The **Enterprise plan** is designed for organizations that need unlimited scale, the highest security controls, custom contractual terms, and a white-glove support experience. Enterprise customers get a dedicated success manager, custom API rate limits, advanced RBAC with scoped roles, configurable data retention, scheduled data exports, and a 99.99% uptime SLA.

Enterprise is ideal for:
- **Large organizations** with hundreds or thousands of users across many workspaces who need centralized governance.
- **Regulated industries** (finance, healthcare, government) that require custom data retention policies, extended audit logs, and stricter compliance documentation.
- **Teams with custom integration needs** who want Envoy's engineering team involved in building or supporting bespoke connectors.
- **Procurement-driven buyers** who require MSA/DPA agreements, custom invoicing, or volume discounts.
