Thumbtack connects customers with local professionals across a wide range of services — from house cleaning to wedding photography. Envoy is built for a different job: **managing a project and reaching out to contacts on your terms**.

Here's an honest comparison to help you decide what fits.

## At a glance

| | **Envoy** | **Thumbtack** |
|---|-----------|---------------|
| **Primary model** | Project workspace + direct outreach | Request quotes from local pros |
| **Typical flow** | Search contacts → plan project → email from your inbox | Describe job → receive quotes → hire |
| **Best for** | Multi-step projects with several contacts | Finding and comparing pros for a specific task |
| **Contact selection** | You choose who to add to your project | Pros opt in to respond to your request |
| **Communication** | Your Gmail or Outlook | Thumbtack messaging / platform tools |
| **Pricing to you** | Free to search; sign in with Google or Outlook | Free for customers; pros pay for leads |
| **AI assistance** | Project chat + outreach draft revision | Platform matching and pro responses |
| **Search before signup** | Yes — homepage contact discovery | Thumbtack account flow for requests |

## What Thumbtack does well

Thumbtack shines when you have a **defined task** and want **competitive quotes** from interested local pros:

- Wide coverage across service categories
- Simple "tell us what you need" intake
- Pros come to you with pricing and availability
- Useful for one-off jobs where comparison shopping is the goal

Examples: hiring a photographer, booking a cleaner, getting quotes for a fence install.

## What Envoy does differently

Envoy optimizes for **ownership of the process** — especially when a job is really a **project**:

### Discovery on your timeline

Start on the [Envoy homepage](https://app.hello-envoy.com) without an account. Describe what you're planning, enter your ZIP or postal code, and browse contacts grouped by category. Your selections save until you're ready to register.

### Projects, not one-off requests

Thumbtack requests are built around a single job description. Envoy projects hold **details, contacts, chat history, and outreach** together — so a restaurant build-out or company event doesn't fragment into disconnected threads.

### Direct outreach from your email

Instead of waiting for pros to buy your lead and respond in-platform, Envoy helps you **draft and send** introductory emails from your own account. You approve every message. Replies land in your inbox.

### AI for planning and writing

Use **Chat** to think through scope before contacting anyone. Use **Revise** on outreach drafts to adjust tone, length, or missing details — without starting from a blank email every time.

## When to use Thumbtack

Choose **Thumbtack** when:

- You want **multiple quotes** for a well-defined single service
- You're comfortable hiring through a marketplace workflow
- Speed of matched responses matters more than long-term project context
- The job is unlikely to involve ongoing multi-vendor coordination

## When to use Envoy

Choose **Envoy** when:

- You're coordinating **several contacts** for one initiative
- You want to **initiate outreach** rather than post and wait
- Project details (timeline, budget, goals, location) need to stay **centralized**
- You prefer email conversations in **your own inbox**
- You want to evaluate contacts **before creating an account**

## Side-by-side example

**Scenario:** Opening a second location for your business — lease negotiation support, contractor, signage, IT setup.

| Step | Thumbtack approach | Envoy approach |
|------|-------------------|----------------|
| Find pros | Separate requests per category | One project; search and attach multiple contacts |
| Share context | Re-enter job details per request | Project description, goals, and dates stored once |
| Communicate | Platform messages per pro | Outreach threads from your email |
| Follow up | Return to each Thumbtack thread | Replies in your inbox; Outreach tab tracks threads |

For a single quote — e.g., "install one door" — Thumbtack may be faster. For the full opening — Envoy keeps the pieces connected.

## Can you use both?

Absolutely. Thumbtack can surface pros or validate pricing; Envoy can be where you **run the larger play** — shortlist, plan, and reach out with full project context.
