## The problem with "post and wait"

Traditional marketplaces ask you to submit a request and wait for pros to come to you. That works for simple one-off jobs. It breaks down when:

- You're coordinating **multiple trades** for one project
- You want to **choose who you contact**, not whoever buys the lead
- You need **context preserved** across weeks of planning and follow-up
- You want replies in **your email**, not buried in a platform inbox

Envoy flips the model. You discover contacts, build your shortlist, plan the work, and reach out on your terms.

## How Envoy fits your workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discover">
    Search by project description and location on the homepage before you sign up, or during project creation from **Dashboard**. Review contacts with clear badges like **Onboarded to Envoy** or **Unverified listing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize">
    Create a project, attach contacts, and capture the details pros actually need — location, dates, budget, goals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan">
    Use **Chat** to refine scope and decide what to ask before you send the first email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reach out">
    Draft, revise, and send outreach from your connected inbox. Follow up in the same thread when replies arrive.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who Envoy is for

**Homeowners and renters** planning renovations, repairs, or upgrades who want to run the process — not hand it off to an algorithm.

**Small business owners** opening locations, upgrading facilities, or coordinating vendors for an event or launch.

**Anyone managing a multi-vendor project** who is tired of repeating the same brief in five different apps.
