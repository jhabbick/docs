## Who Uses Envoy

Envoy is designed to serve teams of every shape and size. Here's how different organizations put it to work:

**Engineering Teams** use Envoy's REST API and webhook system to automate provisioning, sync workspace data with internal tooling, and build custom integrations without maintaining fragile scripts. The developer-first design means your engineers spend time building products, not fighting with platform limitations.

**Operations Teams** rely on Envoy's native integrations with Slack, Jira, and Salesforce to keep cross-functional workflows moving. Role-based access control and audit logs give ops leaders visibility and governance over who can do what — and a clear record when questions arise.

**Startups** appreciate Envoy's free Starter plan and transparent upgrade path. You can launch with zero cost, grow into the Growth plan when you're ready, and never worry about being locked into a pricing model that doesn't match your stage.

**Enterprises** trust Envoy because it meets their security and compliance bar from day one. SOC 2 Type II certification, SAML/OIDC SSO, custom roles, and a dedicated success team mean enterprise procurement and security reviews move faster and close sooner.
