The Envoy team ships fast — and we want to make sure you never miss a change that could save your team time, reduce friction, or open up new workflows. This post rounds up our most recent feature releases, the behind-the-scenes improvements that make Envoy faster and more reliable, and an early look at what's landing next quarter. Whether you're on the Starter plan or running an Enterprise workspace, there's something here for you.

---

## New Features

### 1. Bulk Member Import

Onboarding a new department or migrating from another platform? The new **Bulk Member Import** tool lets workspace admins upload a CSV file to invite up to 500 members in a single operation. Each row in the CSV can specify a name, email address, and role, so your team lands in Envoy with the right permissions from day one — no manual clicking required.

This feature is available on all plans. Head to **Settings → Members → Import** to try it now.

### 2. Webhook Event Filtering

Webhook subscribers used to receive every event fired from a workspace — useful in some cases, noisy in most. With **Webhook Event Filtering**, you can now scope each webhook endpoint to receive only the event types you care about (for example, `member.created` or `role.updated`) and optionally restrict delivery to a specific workspace. This means less processing on your end, cleaner logs, and more reliable automation pipelines.

Event filtering is available to all Growth and Enterprise customers. See the [Integrations guide](/help-center/concepts/integrations) for details on webhook configuration.

### 3. Custom Role Builder *(Enterprise)*

Role-based access control is only as powerful as the roles you can define. The new **Custom Role Builder** gives Enterprise workspace owners a visual interface to create granular permission sets — down to the individual action level — without filing a support ticket or waiting for an engineering sprint. Mix and match permissions across resources like members, integrations, audit logs, and billing, then save your role and assign it immediately.

Custom roles are fully compatible with SSO attribute mapping, so you can push role assignments directly from your identity provider.

## Improvements

Beyond new features, we've been focused on making the Envoy you already use feel faster and more polished.

- **Faster API Response Times** — We've significantly reduced latency across our core API endpoints. The p99 response time has dropped from 250 ms to 80 ms, a 68% improvement. For teams running high-frequency automations or real-time integrations, this should be immediately noticeable.

- **Redesigned Integration Directory** — The integrations page has been rebuilt from scratch with a new search bar, category filters, and cleaner connection status indicators. Finding and managing your connected apps — whether that's Slack, GitHub, Jira, or Zapier — now takes a fraction of the time it used to.

- **New Audit Log Export Filters** — Audit log exports now support date-range filtering, event-type filtering, and actor filtering before you download. This makes compliance reviews and incident investigations far more efficient, especially for large workspaces with high event volumes.

## Coming Soon

We're always building. Here's a glimpse at what's on the near-term roadmap:

1. **Native Salesforce Integration (Q3)** — Sync Envoy workspace membership and role changes directly with Salesforce records. This will be especially useful for customer-facing teams that mirror their Salesforce org structure in Envoy.

2. **AI-Powered Activity Summaries** — Get a weekly digest that uses AI to surface the most significant activity in your workspace — new integrations, role changes, spike in API usage — so you can stay informed without reviewing raw logs.

Both features are currently in private beta. If you'd like early access, reach out to your account manager or contact our support team.
