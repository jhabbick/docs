Angi (formerly Angie's List) is one of the best-known home services marketplaces in the U.S. Envoy takes a different approach — built for people who want to **run a project**, not just **request a quote**.

This page explains where each platform fits so you can choose the right tool for your situation.

## At a glance

| | **Envoy** | **Angi** |
|---|-----------|----------|
| **Primary model** | Project workspace + direct outreach | Marketplace connecting homeowners with pros |
| **Who initiates contact** | You choose contacts and reach out | Pros often respond to your posted request |
| **Best for** | Multi-vendor projects you manage end-to-end | Finding a pro for a defined home service job |
| **Project context** | Title, location, budget, deadline, and contacts in one place | Job request focused on a single service category |
| **Communication** | Email from your Gmail or Outlook | Platform-mediated leads and messaging |
| **Envoy assistance** | Project-scoped **Chat** + draft outreach | Varies by Angi product/experience |
| **Search before signup** | Yes — homepage contact search | Angi account typically required earlier in flow |
| **Pro economics** | Direct relationship; no lead fees to you | Pros may pay for leads through Angi's model |

## What Angi does well

Angi excels when you have a **clear, single-category home need** — a plumber for a leak, an electrician for an outlet, a roofer for a repair — and you want access to a large pool of reviewed local pros without building a project plan yourself.

Strengths include:

- Broad directory of home service categories
- Established brand recognition in home services
- Reviews and ratings to evaluate pros
- Familiar "find a pro" flow for homeowners

## What Envoy does differently

Envoy is built for the **planning and coordination layer** that marketplaces don't always cover well:

### You pick the contacts

On Envoy's homepage, describe your project and search by location. Review results, check badges like **Onboarded to Envoy**, and select up to 8 contacts before you even sign up. You're building a shortlist — not posting a job and waiting to see who bites.

### Your project stays organized

A kitchen remodel isn't one Angi category — it's cabinets, counters, plumbing, electrical, and maybe a GC. Envoy keeps contacts, details, and conversation context attached to **one project**, not scattered across separate requests.

### Outreach comes from you

Envoy drafts emails; you approve and send from **your own inbox**. When a contact replies, the thread is yours. There's no marketplace middle layer deciding who gets your message or when.

### Envoy helps you plan, not just match

The **Chat** tab helps you think through scope, priorities, and questions before outreach. **Revise with Envoy** lets you tighten drafts before you hit send.

## When to use Angi

Choose **Angi** when:

- You need one type of home service pro quickly
- Reviews and Angi's pro network are your primary decision tools
- You prefer a marketplace to route leads to available pros
- The job is straightforward and doesn't require multi-vendor coordination

## When to use Envoy

Choose **Envoy** when:

- You're planning a project that spans **multiple trades or phases**
- You want to **control who you contact** and what you say to them
- You need project details, contacts, and email threads in **one workspace**
- You want to search for contacts **before committing to an account**
- Keeping conversations in **your email** matters for follow-through

## Can you use both?

Yes. Many people use a marketplace like Angi to discover or validate a pro, and Envoy to **coordinate a larger initiative** — especially when the same project involves several contacts, a real timeline, and ongoing email follow-up.
