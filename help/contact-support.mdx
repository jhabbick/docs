When you run into an issue or have a question that isn't answered in our documentation, the Envoy support team is here to help. This page explains the support channels available for each plan, how to submit a ticket effectively, what response times to expect, and where to find community resources.

## Support Channels by Plan

The support options available to you depend on your Envoy plan. Here's an overview:

| Plan | Channels Available |
|------|--------------------|
| **Starter (Free)** | Email support |
| **Growth ($49/mo)** | Email support + Live chat (in-app) |
| **Enterprise (Custom)** | Email + Live chat + Dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM) + Phone support |

To access live chat on the Growth or Enterprise plan, click the **Help** icon in the bottom-right corner of any page in the Envoy dashboard.

## How to Submit a Support Ticket

Email support is available to all customers. To get the fastest resolution, follow these steps when submitting a ticket:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Email the support team">
    Send your request to [support@envoy.com](mailto:support@envoy.com). Use a clear, descriptive subject line — for example, "API returning 403 errors on workspace update endpoint" rather than just "API issue."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include your workspace details">
    In the body of your email, include your **workspace ID** (found under **Settings → General**) and the **email address** associated with your account. This allows us to look up your account immediately without back-and-forth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the issue clearly">
    Explain what you expected to happen, what actually happened, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Include relevant details such as browser and OS version, integration names, API endpoint URLs, and any error messages you've received.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach supporting information">
    Include screenshots, screen recordings, log excerpts, or exported data that illustrate the problem. The more context you provide, the faster we can investigate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
    You'll receive an automated confirmation email with a ticket number. Keep this for reference. A support agent will follow up within the timeframe for your plan (see below).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Expected Response Times

Response times vary by plan and the severity of the issue:

| Plan | Standard Response Time | Urgent / Outage Response |
|------|------------------------|--------------------------|
| **Starter** | Within 3 business days | Within 1 business day |
| **Growth** | Within 1 business day | Within 4 business hours |
| **Enterprise** | Within 4 business hours | Within 1 hour (via CSM or phone) |

Business hours are Monday–Friday, 9 AM–6 PM ET. Enterprise customers with active SLA agreements may have custom response time commitments specified in their contract.

<Note>
  When contacting support, always include your **workspace ID** to help us locate your account quickly. You can find your workspace ID under **Settings → General**. 

  **Never share your API key publicly** — not in community forum posts, GitHub issues, screenshots, or anywhere else. Your API key grants full access to your workspace. If you believe your key has been exposed, revoke it immediately at **Settings → API → API Keys** and generate a new one before contacting support.
</Note>

- **[API Reference](https://api.envoy.com/v1/docs)** — Full technical documentation for every Envoy API endpoint, including request/response schemas, authentication details, and code examples.
- **[Video Tutorials](https://envoy.com/tutorials)** — Step-by-step video walkthroughs for common tasks like setting up integrations, managing roles, and using the API.
