Understanding how billing works helps you get the most out of your Envoy subscription. This page answers the most common questions about plans, payment methods, invoices, mid-cycle changes, and what happens when you cancel or need a refund.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What plans does Envoy offer?">
    Envoy offers three plans designed to fit teams of every size:

    | Plan | Price | Key Features |
    |------|-------|--------------|
    | **Starter** | Free | 1 workspace, core features, email support |
    | **Growth** | $49/mo | Up to 10 workspaces, all integrations, email + chat support |
    | **Enterprise** | Custom pricing | Unlimited workspaces, SSO, dedicated CSM, SLA, custom contracts |

    All paid plans are available on monthly or annual billing. Annual billing provides a discount equivalent to two months free. You can compare plans in detail at **Settings → Billing → Compare Plans**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change plans at any time?">
    Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from **Settings → Billing**.

    - **Upgrading** takes effect immediately, and you'll be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your current billing cycle.
    - **Downgrading** takes effect at the end of your current billing period. Your account will retain Growth or Enterprise features until that date.

    If you downgrade and your usage exceeds the limits of the lower tier (for example, more than one workspace on Starter), you'll be prompted to resolve those conflicts before the downgrade is finalized.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does billing work for annual plans?">
    When you choose annual billing, you pay for a full year upfront at a discounted rate — equivalent to getting two months free compared to monthly billing. For example, the Growth plan at $49/mo billed monthly comes to $588/year, while annual billing is charged at $490/year.

    Annual subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each 12-month period. You'll receive a renewal reminder by email 30 days before your renewal date. To switch between monthly and annual billing, go to **Settings → Billing → Manage Subscription**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
    Envoy accepts the following payment methods for self-serve plans (Starter and Growth):

    - Major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
    - ACH bank transfers (US accounts only)

    Enterprise customers may also pay by invoice with net-30 terms. All payments are processed securely through Stripe. Envoy does not store your full card details — only a tokenized reference.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get an invoice?">
    Invoices are generated automatically after each successful payment and sent to the billing email address on file. To view or download past invoices:

    1. Go to **Settings → Billing**.
    2. Click the **Invoices** tab.
    3. Select any invoice and click **Download PDF**.

    To update your billing email or add a billing address and tax ID to your invoices, click **Edit Billing Details** on the same page. Enterprise customers can request custom invoice formats through their Customer Success Manager.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I add seats mid-cycle?">
    Yes. You can add seats to your plan at any time from **Settings → Billing → Manage Seats**. When you add seats mid-cycle, you'll be charged a prorated amount for the remaining days in your current billing period. The new seats are available immediately after the charge is processed.

    Removing seats takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle — you won't receive a credit for the unused portion of the current period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I cancel?">
    When you cancel your Envoy subscription:

    - Your plan will remain active until the end of your current billing period.
    - After that date, your account will automatically move to the **Starter (Free)** plan.
    - Your data is retained, but access to paid features (additional workspaces, integrations, etc.) will be restricted.
    - Workspaces exceeding the Starter limit will be locked (read-only) and can be deleted or exported.

    To cancel, go to **Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription** and follow the prompts. If you change your mind, you can reactivate a paid plan at any time before your billing period ends to avoid any interruption.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you offer refunds?">
    Envoy does not offer refunds for partial billing periods on monthly plans. For annual plans, refunds may be issued within 14 days of the initial purchase or renewal if you have not materially used the service during that period — contact [support@envoy.com](mailto:support@envoy.com) to request a review.

    If you were charged in error or experienced a billing issue, please reach out to our support team and we'll make it right.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a discount for nonprofits or education?">
    Yes. Envoy offers discounted pricing for qualifying nonprofit organizations and educational institutions. To apply:

    1. Email [support@envoy.com](mailto:support@envoy.com) with the subject line **"Nonprofit/Education Discount Request"**.
    2. Include your organization's name, website, and a brief description of how you use (or plan to use) Envoy.
    3. Attach proof of nonprofit status (e.g., IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter) or educational institution verification.

    Our team typically reviews applications within 3–5 business days. Approved organizations receive a discount applied directly to their subscription.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact billing support?">
    For billing questions, you have several options depending on your plan:

    - **All plans:** Email [support@envoy.com](mailto:support@envoy.com) with "Billing" in the subject line.
    - **Growth plan:** Use the live chat widget in the Envoy dashboard for faster responses.
    - **Enterprise plan:** Contact your dedicated Customer Success Manager directly or call your assigned support number.

    Our billing support team is available Monday–Friday, 9 AM–6 PM ET. For urgent billing issues outside these hours, email us and we'll respond on the next business day.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
